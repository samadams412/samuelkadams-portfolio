## Problem

As part of a six-person team, the assignment was to assess the cybersecurity posture of a simulated small R&D nonprofit against a recognized framework, rather than an informal checklist.

## Approach

The team applied NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 and NISTIR 7621 guidance, evaluating physical access controls, network segmentation, and remote-access policy. Findings were mapped to CSF functions and paired with mitigations sized for a resource-constrained organization, and the team produced network topology and risk-mapping diagrams to communicate results to non-technical stakeholders.

## Outcome

The assessment surfaced real gaps, including the absence of multi-factor authentication on VPN access and reliance on an end-of-life operating system in production, alongside genuine strengths like automated nightly backups and full-disk encryption. The full report runs about 30 pages; this write-up summarizes it rather than reproducing it.
