## Problem

A small business needed an e-commerce site built and shipped for real customers, not a demo. The client owned the relationship end to end, from initial requirements through post-launch support.

## Approach

The site is built on WordPress and WooCommerce on top of a pre-built theme, customized with PHP, CSS, and JavaScript to match the client's branding and the functionality they needed. Stripe handles payment processing, and WooCommerce manages order and inventory tracking. The build was tested across browsers and screen sizes, since most of the client's traffic comes through mobile.

## Outcome

The site is live and in production, handling real orders for a real client. There's no public repo for this one since it's a managed WordPress install, not a standalone codebase, and no financial specifics are shared here out of respect for the client relationship.
